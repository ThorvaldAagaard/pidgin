General approach
Natural. Four-card suits, light openings, weak twos in all four suits, one wide strong 1NT for big hands of any shape. No artificial bids. The 1NT response to a suit opening is the strong bid; new suits at the two level are weak. A new suit shows 4+ cards (99.75% of 2.46 million first bids of a suit); a raise can be 3 cards.

Special doubles

Doubles are for penalty. No takeout doubles. At levels 1–3 the doubler holds their suit (4+ cards 91% / 64% / 60%); a takeout shape (2 or fewer in their suit, 3+ in every unbid suit) is 0.3% / 7% / 7%. At the 4 level and up the doubler often does not hold the suit (4+ only 11–34%): the double says "they will go down". Partner leaves it in 38% at the 1 level, 73–82% at the 4 level and up.
Examples: 1♠–X shows 4.7 spades on average (4+ 99%), 13–21 HCP; 1♥–P–P–X shows 4+ hearts (100%); 2♥–X 4+ hearts 90%.

Slam conventions

4NTNot Blackwood: pick a slam. Partner names a real suit at the 5 level (76%; 4+ cards 93–98%) or bids 5NT (11%). Aces held are the same whatever the reply (1–2 aces 74–77% for each 5-level suit). The auction ends in slam 82% of the time.
