Opening bids compared

What six bidders open on the same random hands (the last 25,000 deals of our test file, all four hands). Each bot makes its normal choice, as in a match. Pick a bidder, a seat and the vulnerability. "Vulnerable" means the opener's side is vulnerable.

Bidders: Pidgin V3.1 (VieNNa, the system in the article), Pidgin V2 (the version before) and Pidgin V1 (our first version); BRL, the FSP model from Kita et al. 2024; BEN's SAYC and 2/1 networks (lorserker/ben, network only, no search). HCP = high card points.

Results against each other

Bidding only, scored double dummy, IMPs per board for the row bidder, from earlier match records (no new runs). Pidgin V3.1 rows: 10,000 test deals × 16 boards = 160,000 boards per match, 95% interval. Pidgin V2 vs V1 and Pidgin V1 vs BRL: 160,000 boards each, ± = one standard error (V1 vs BRL on a different deal set). Empty = never played.
